How a project runs

Six stages, in this order, every time.

Skipping a stage is where most local projects go wrong. Nothing here starts until the stage before it is signed off.

01

Site Visit & Feasibility

Soil, access and plot orientation assessed in person before any design work begins.

02

Design & Structural Drawings

Layout, elevation and structural drawings prepared and reviewed with you line by line.

03

Approvals & Documentation

Municipal building plan approval and any statutory clearances filed and tracked to completion.

04

Costing & Fixed Quote

A full bill of quantities is priced and shared — this becomes the number you're held to.

05

Execution & Supervision

Construction proceeds in scheduled milestones, with daily site checks against the drawings.

06

Quality Check & Handover

Final inspection, snag list closure, and documentation handed over with the keys.

Transparent budgeting

You see the same numbers I work from.

Every quote is built as an itemised bill of quantities — not a lump sum. Payments are tied to completed milestones, not the calendar.

Itemised bill of quantities

Every material, brand, grade and rate listed before work starts — not summarised into one figure.

Milestone-based payment

You pay for work completed and inspected — foundation, structure, roofing, finishing — not on a fixed monthly date.

Written approval for any change

If a variation affects cost, you approve it in writing first. No verbal additions billed after the fact.

Weekly progress against schedule

A running comparison of planned vs. actual progress, shared with you every week — not only at milestones.
